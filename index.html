The Atelier

One pair of hands,
a lot of patience

I’m Paz. I’ve been sewing since I was small — taking apart the things I owned to understand how they were put together, long before I was allowed near the machine. Today I make clothes to measure from a little studio in Geneva: dresses, tailoring, alterations, and the occasional impossible request.

The dress in the photograph is one of mine. Nothing about it is complicated — it’s a good pattern, honest fabric and time taken over the finishing. That’s the whole secret, and it’s exactly what I teach.

New in Geneva

From flat pattern
to finished garment

Six evenings, six people, one machine each. We start with a pattern and a length of fabric and we don’t stop until you have something you’d actually wear out of the house. No experience needed — most people arrive having never threaded a bobbin.

  1. 01

    Reading & adjusting a pattern

    Measurements, sizing, and how to change a pattern so it fits you.

  2. 02

    Fabric, cutting & the machine

    Choosing cloth, laying it out, cutting cleanly, and the stitches you’ll actually use.

  3. 03

    Assembly

    Seams, darts, gathers, a zip or a tie closure — the garment starts to exist.

  4. 04

    Fitting & finishing

    Hems, edges, the small details that separate handmade from homemade.
