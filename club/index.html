Geneva Sewing Club

Join us at the Geneva Sewing Club, a relaxed weekly sewing session in Geneva and a chance to make time for your creative projects at the end of the week.

Whether you’re looking to get started with sewing, build your confidence, work on a project you’ve been putting off, or simply enjoy sewing alongside other people, you’re very welcome. You don’t need to be an experienced sewist, basic sewing knowledge is enough, and I’ll be there to guide you whenever you need a hand.

Bring your own project, work at your own pace, ask questions, exchange ideas, and enjoy the simple pleasure of making things together. I’ll be there to help with techniques, troubleshoot tricky bits, and share tips along the way.

As a self-taught sewist, I know how exciting (and sometimes intimidating!) it can be to figure things out on your own. I created the Geneva Sewing Club as a space to make that process more enjoyable, because sewing is simply better when you don't have to do it alone.

My hope is to create a warm, welcoming community around sewing, while keeping the craft alive and celebrating the creativity, skills, and connection that come with making things with your own hands.

A monthly project

Every month, we'll choose a sewing pattern together and work on it as a group over four or five consecutive sessions.

We'll vote on the pattern at the beginning of each month on our WhatsApp group, and I'll guide you through the different stages of the project, from understanding the pattern and preparing your fabric to sewing, fitting and finishing your garment.

You’re absolutely welcome to join even if you don't want to make the collective project. You can bring your own project and use the sessions as dedicated time to sew, with help and advice whenever you need it.

What to bring

For each monthly cycle, we'll vote together on a sewing pattern to work on over the four/five sessions. You’re welcome to join the collective project, or bring your own sewing project and work on it at your own pace.

For your own project, please bring:

  • Your sewing machine
  • Your fabric and any other materials specific to your project

Don't have a sewing machine? We have two sewing machines available for participants to try, depending on availability. Please mention this when signing up, as they are shared and cannot be guaranteed for every session.

You don't need to carry your machine around in your arms, a sewing machine bag, trolley or small suitcase works perfectly!

Need fabric? I can also prepare fabric for your project. This is available at an additional cost, and you can choose your fabric when signing up.

Languages

Geneva is wonderfully multicultural, and I want the sewing club to reflect that. Sessions are held in English, French and Spanish, and you're welcome to contact me in any of these languages.
